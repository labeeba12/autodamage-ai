About the Project

Built to make car damage
assessment smarter.

AutoDamage AI is an intelligent system that combines three fine-tuned deep learning models to automatically detect car damage, identify affected parts, and estimate repair costs — built specifically for the Pakistani automotive market.

AutoDamage AI addresses a real gap in the Pakistani automotive industry — the lack of fast, objective, and data-driven vehicle damage assessment. Traditional methods rely on manual inspection by workshop staff, which is time-consuming, inconsistent, and prone to human error.

Our system uses two specialized YOLOv11 models working alongside a targeted XGBoost Regressor in a unified pipeline: a parts segmentation model that maps the vehicle structure, a damage detection model that identifies and localizes damage, and a localized cost engine that processes the user-selected vehicle profile. Together, they calculate highly accurate, market-specific repair estimates in seconds.

Inference Pipeline
Image Validation
Uploaded image is validated for minimum resolution (224×224px), file integrity, and blur score using Laplacian variance. Low quality images are rejected with a clear message.
cv2 · Laplacian
Three-Model Inference
All core processing engines execute on the validated image and user input. The parts model produces segmentation masks for 22 car parts, the damage model detects and segments 6 damage types, and the XGBoost cost engine scales the final repair pricing based on the selected vehicle profile.
YOLOv11n-seg · YOLOv11m-seg · XGBoost
Overlap Engine
Each damage mask is matched to the most overlapping car part mask using IoU (Intersection over Union) scoring. This determines which part was damaged and where on the vehicle it occurred.
IoU · Mask Overlap
Severity & Cost Calculation
Each matched damage is graded by severity (minor/moderate/severe) based on area ratio. A decision engine determines repair vs replace action. XGBoost-enhanced pricing with baseline PKR cost tables produces the final estimate range.
XGBoost · Rule-based Pricing
Report Generation
A structured JSON report is returned via the FastAPI endpoint, accompanied by a before/after visualization and a top-down damage heatmap showing affected zones color-coded by severity.
FastAPI · OpenCV · JSON
